§ III · Data Sources

Sources & Methodology

Open data OpenStreetMap Contributors. Waterways, water bodies, and points of interest queried via Overpass API. Includes rivers, streams, canals, drains, and ditches. License: ODbL. openstreetmap.org

FEMA FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (NFHL). Flood hazard zones (100-year, 500-year, floodways) queried via ArcGIS REST services. Official regulatory flood maps for the National Flood Insurance Program. msc.fema.gov

USGS USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset (WBD). HUC12 subwatershed boundaries queried via National Map ArcGIS REST services. Hydrologic unit codes and watershed names. usgs.gov/wbd

NCGS North Carolina Geological Survey. Landslide hazard maps (where available in NC). Henderson County Slope Movement Hazard Maps (Wooten et al. 2011, DDS-GHMS-5). For other states, refer to the respective state geological survey. USGS national landslide susceptibility data is available at usgs.gov/landslide-hazards.

Esri / USDA Esri World Imagery & Wayback. Satellite basemap and historical imagery (2019—2025). NAIP imagery integrated in select Wayback releases for US coverage at 0.6—1.0 m resolution. livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback

Open data Nominatim (OSM). Geocoding for address/place name resolution. nominatim.org

Open data Overture Maps Foundation. For deeper analysis, additional place, building, and infrastructure layers available via GeoParquet on S3. Accessible via DuckDB + httpfs. overturemaps.org

Coordinate reference: EPSG:4326. Search radius: 3,218 m (~2 miles). FEMA NFHL data is regulatory-grade but may not reflect recent flood events. Landslide hazard data availability varies by state.

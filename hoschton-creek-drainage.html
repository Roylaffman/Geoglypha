§ II · Watershed Analysis

Drainage Context

The property sits in the upper drainage basin of Indian Creek, a named tributary of the North Oconee River in Jackson County. The immediate area is drained by a network of small unnamed tributaries that flow generally northwest-to-southeast before joining Indian Creek approximately 2.5 km to the east-southeast.

Stream Network at Property

OSM data (sourced from USGS NHD at 1:24,000 scale) maps two unnamed stream segments in the direct vicinity of the property. The primary concern is OSM Way 1053938641, which passes approximately 71 m north of the property building location. This stream receives drainage from OSM Way 34448764 (a longer unnamed tributary originating approximately 700 m to the north-northwest) via a culvert crossing.

The stream network in this area includes several culvert crossings at road and subdivision infrastructure, which alter natural flow paths. Any new pond constructed within this drainage network — particularly one that intercepts the unnamed tributary — would redirect velocity and volume downstream, potentially creating erosive conditions at the outlet.

§ IV · Elevation & LiDAR

Topographic Data

Jackson County is covered by the 2018–2019 USGS 3DEP GA Statewide LiDAR project (task order 140G0218F0420), providing 1-meter bare-earth DEMs. This is sufficient resolution to model pre- and post-modification flow direction and identify where the natural drainage path of the unnamed creek would run absent the pond.

Access LiDAR and Topo Data

  • NOAA Digital Coast Data Access Viewer — coast.noaa.gov/dataviewer — Draw a custom AOI around the property, download the 1-meter DEM clip directly. Free, no account needed.
  • OpenTopography GA Statewide 2018 — portal.opentopography.org — Process the LiDAR point cloud for the AOI; generate DEMs and hillshades on-demand.
  • USGS LiDAR Explorer — usgs.gov/tools/lidarexplorer — Confirm tile coverage for Jackson County; download tiles by map selection.
  • USGS National Map Viewer — apps.nationalmap.gov/viewer — Stream the 1/3 arc-second (10m) or 1-meter 3DEP elevation layers directly; export contours for the property area.

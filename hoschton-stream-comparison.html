§ I · Purpose

Why Compare Stream Sources

Three authoritative datasets independently map the unnamed creek that passes approximately 71 m north of 368 Joshua Way. Each uses different survey methods, vertex density, and update cycles. Comparing them reveals where the datasets agree on channel position, where they diverge, and which reach each organization considered primary. Together, OSM and NHD trace the complete channel from headwaters to confluence with Indian Creek.

The NHDPlus HR service returned geometry essentially identical to NHD Standard for this reach (sub-meter alignment), so it is shown alongside NHD as a confirmation layer rather than a separate geometry.

Key Finding: Connected Reaches

The OSM way and the NHD perennial segment connect at approximately [34.0935, -83.7462]. The OSM coverage spans the critical reach that passes nearest the property. NHD continues the channel southeast toward Indian Creek. The NHD intermittent segment above extends the headwaters northward, indicating the creek runs dry seasonally in its upper portion before becoming perennial near the OSM start node.

Petra & the Nabataean World

4th century BCE – 106 CE. The Nabataeans carved a desert empire from the Incense Road. This atlas maps the Rose-Red City at Petra alongside the caravan network, trade routes, and inscribed frontiers that made a nomadic people the wealthiest traders in the ancient Near East.

Enclosure Atlas

Göbekli Tepe

c. 9600 BCE. The oldest known monumental architecture on earth, raised by hunter-gatherers a thousand years before the invention of agriculture. This enclosure atlas maps the T-shaped pillar rings of Layer III using whitebox proxy models, tracing each excavated enclosure in the limestone ridge above the Harran plain.

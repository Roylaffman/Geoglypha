Precipitation Analysis

Precipitation in Asheville reached its peak during September 26-27, 2024, with the most significant rainfall occurring during the storm system.

The maximum hourly precipitation of 18.0 mm was recorded at 13:00 on September 27th, coinciding with the lowest barometric pressure and highest wind speeds.

Total precipitation during this 48-hour period was 107.3 mm, accounting for 95% of the total rainfall during the entire 5-day period.

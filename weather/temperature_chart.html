§ Temperature Analysis  —  Asheville, NC · Sept 2024

Temperature Variations

Temperature in Asheville fluctuated between 15.4°C and 23.7°C during the period of September 25-29, 2024.

The highest temperature was recorded on September 25th, while a significant drop occurred on September 27th during a storm system.

After the storm passed, temperatures stabilized with a gradual warming trend toward the end of the period.

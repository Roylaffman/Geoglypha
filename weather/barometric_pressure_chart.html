Barometric Pressure Trends

Barometric pressure in Asheville showed a dramatic drop during the storm system, reaching a minimum of 980.7 hPa on September 27th at 13:00.

This significant pressure drop coincided with the peak wind speeds and heaviest precipitation, marking the passage of the storm's center.

After the storm passed, pressure gradually recovered to normal levels (1005-1010 hPa) over the following 24 hours.

Wind Speed Analysis

Wind speeds in Asheville showed dramatic variations during the storm system, with peak gusts reaching 50.3 km/h on September 27th.

The most significant wind activity occurred between 9:00 and 15:00 on September 27th, coinciding with the storm's center passage.

Wind speeds returned to normal levels (5-15 km/h) after the storm system moved through the area.
